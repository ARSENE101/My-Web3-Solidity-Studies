# My Solidity Studies ⚒️

This repository serves as the central hub for my Solidity and
smart-contract development studies.

Rather than keeping every stage of my learning in one repository,
I separate my work based on the development environment and
workflow I am studying.

This makes it easier to follow my progression from my early
Solidity experiments in Remix to a more structured development
workflow using Foundry.

---

## 🧭 My Learning Journey

My Solidity journey began with **Remix**, where I learned the
fundamentals of Solidity by writing, compiling, deploying, and
interacting with smart contracts directly in the browser.

As my understanding developed, I moved toward **Foundry** to
learn a more complete smart-contract development workflow,
including local blockchain development, testing, scripting,
deployment, RPC communication, and transaction broadcasting.

The two repositories below represent those stages of my journey.
